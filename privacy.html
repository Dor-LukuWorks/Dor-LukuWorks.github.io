Privacy

Your game, your information.

Draft prepared 15 November 2026. Not yet an effective public-release notice.

This notice describes Sweet Rings, operated by Dor Mogev in Singapore. Contact dormogg@gmail.com about privacy or support.

Progress on your device

The game stores your levels, turns, coins, booster inventory, preferences and reward-delivery records on your device. You do not need a game account. We do not operate cloud progress storage or automatically upload gameplay analytics in this version. Your operating system may manage backups and diagnostics under its own settings.

Rewarded advertising and privacy choices

Sweet Rings integrates Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform. When advertising is enabled, these services can process IP address and approximate location, device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, consent preferences, crash information and performance diagnostics to deliver and measure ads, manage privacy choices and prevent abuse.

Our current private QA build uses official Google test ads, which earn no advertising revenue. The planned public version offers optional rewarded ads for coins or extra turns. The final release configuration and store disclosures must be reviewed before this draft is published.

The game requests non-personalized ads and disables the publisher first-party identifier. This does not mean that ads process no data. Google’s services may process information outside your country. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where required, a privacy message asks for your choices before eligible ad requests. You can revisit available choices through Settings → Privacy choices. Ad availability and the kinds of ads that can be served may depend on those choices. Core puzzle play does not require watching a video.

This website

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. We have added no website analytics, cookies or contact form. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement for its hosting practices.

If you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and investigate the problem. Support correspondence is handled through Gmail. Send only what is needed; do not include passwords, tax identifiers or payment details.

Retention and requests

Local game information stays on your device until it is removed or replaced. We cannot recover or remotely delete progress that is only stored on your device. Support messages are retained while needed to handle the request and any applicable recordkeeping obligations. Google handles its own records under its policies.

You may contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of information we hold in support correspondence, or to raise a privacy concern. Available rights depend on your circumstances and applicable law. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

Audience

Sweet Rings is intended for a general audience aged 13 and above, rather than a game directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us. Advertising age handling remains a release review requirement; an intended age range alone does not establish consent eligibility.

Changes

We will update this notice when the game’s data practices change and identify the effective date of the published version.
