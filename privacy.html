Privacy

Your game, your information.

Last updated: 15 November 2026. Covers private testing and the described rewarded-ad features as they become available.

This notice describes Sweet Rings, operated by Dor Mogev in Singapore. Contact dormogg@gmail.com about privacy or support.

Progress on your device

The game stores your levels, turns, coins, booster inventory, preferences and reward-delivery records on your device. You do not need a game account. We do not operate cloud progress storage or automatically upload gameplay analytics in this version. Your operating system may manage backups and diagnostics under its own settings.

Rewarded advertising and privacy choices

Sweet Rings integrates Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform. When advertising is enabled, these services can process IP address and approximate location, device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, consent preferences, crash information and performance diagnostics to deliver and measure ads, manage privacy choices and prevent abuse.

Our current private QA build uses official Google test ads, which earn no advertising revenue. Rewarded-ad versions offer optional videos for coins or extra turns. Test ads are identified as tests; they do not generate advertising revenue. Public availability of the game is separate from this notice.

The game requests non-personalized ads and disables the publisher first-party identifier and the SDK’s automatic approximate-location collection. IP-derived location and other SDK processing may still occur. The game does not request location permission or Apple’s advertising-tracking permission. This does not mean that ads process no data. Google and advertising partners may receive and process ad-related information. The regional privacy message identifies applicable partners and purposes. Google operates its advertising services under its own data policies. These services may process information outside your country, including in the United States; Google describes its transfer safeguards in its privacy policy. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where required, a privacy message asks for your choices before eligible ad requests. You can revisit available choices through Settings → Privacy choices. You may refuse consent or change available choices there. In covered US states, this includes available sale/sharing opt-out choices. Ad availability and the kinds of ads that can be served may depend on those choices. We request consent where required for advertising-related storage and processing; the regional message explains the purposes and applicable legal bases. Core puzzle play does not require watching a video.

This website

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. We have added no website analytics, cookies or contact form. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement for its hosting practices.

If you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and investigate the problem. Support correspondence is handled through Gmail. Send only what is needed; do not include passwords, tax identifiers or payment details.

Retention and requests

Local game information stays on your device until it is removed or replaced. We cannot recover or remotely delete progress that is only stored on your device. Support messages are retained while needed to handle the request and any applicable recordkeeping obligations. Google handles its own records under its policies.

You may contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of information we hold in support correspondence, or to raise a privacy concern. Available rights depend on your circumstances and applicable law and may include access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction or objection, withdrawal of consent and complaints to the relevant data-protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not change the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

Audience

Sweet Rings is intended for a general audience aged 13 and above, rather than a game directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us. The age-gated version asks for a coarse range, not a date of birth. The choice is saved on the device. Players under 16, or who skip the choice, can play without advertising; the game does not initialize its ad or consent SDK for them through its advertising integration. Players aged 16–17 use Google’s teen ad treatment, which sends a restricted-age treatment signal with eligible ad requests. Eligible older players may choose rewarded videos after applicable privacy choices. If you skipped the choice, Age range in Settings lets you provide it later. A saved range is not automatically changed as you get older; contact support about an incorrect choice.

Changes

We will update this notice when the game’s data practices change and identify the updated date. Material changes will also be explained in the app where required.
