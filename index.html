Quick answers

How are turns counted?

Moving to a different ring uses a turn. You can keep adjusting the selected ring during that turn. Watch the counter and plan your next choice.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress, coins, boosters and settings are saved on your device. There is no Sweet Rings account or automatic game sync between devices. Deleting the app can remove your progress, so contact us before reinstalling to solve a problem.

Why is a reward video unavailable?

Reward videos are available only to eligible players aged 16 or above. A video may also be unavailable because of your connection, privacy choices, the daily limit or the ad provider. Extra-turn recovery is available once per attempt when an ad can play. An unavailable or uncompleted video gives no reward.

I watched a video but did not receive a reward.

Wait for the ad to finish and close, then check the reward. If it still does not appear, reopen the app once. If that does not help, contact support with the approximate time and reward type. Please do not uninstall the app.

How do I change sound or privacy choices?

Open Settings for the separate Music and Sound controls. Privacy choices appears there when the ad consent provider requires it.
